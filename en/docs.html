Documentation

How to use ExtMusic 1.4.2, with the exact names of every option in the app.

The app is in Spanish only. Each option is written as it appears on your phone, in Spanish, followed by its translation in italics. Settings are under Ajustes (Settings).

1Getting started

Download the APK from GitHub and install it: the steps are in Versions → Install. No account is needed. When you first use the app it will ask you, only when needed, for:

Table 1. What the app asks for and why
RequestWhenWhat for
NotificationsThe first time.Playback controls and notices about downloads and new versions.
Background playbackWhen the app needs it.So the music keeps playing with the app closed. You can choose «Ahora no» (Not now).
Access to musicWhen you open the Local tab.Read the music stored on your phone.

The app has four tabs: Inicio (Home), Buscar (Search), Local and Biblioteca (Library). Ajustes (Settings) is the gear on Home.

2Listening

  • Search: songs, artists and albums from YouTube Music, with the Songs and Artists filters. No sign-in.
  • Queue: tap «A continuación» (Up next) in the player. Drag a song's handle to reorder it, or remove it (you can undo).
  • Reproducción automática (Autoplay) (Ajustes → Reproducción): when the queue ends, it keeps playing similar songs. It can also be switched from the queue itself.
  • Sleep timer: Temporizador de sueño (Sleep timer) in the player's ⋮ menu.
  • Barajar al reproducir una lista (Shuffle when playing a list): the song you pick plays first and the rest in random order.
  • Ocultar canciones explícitas (Hide explicit songs): they don't appear in searches, recommendations or lists.
  • Seguir sonando al cerrar la app (Keep playing when the app is closed): if you turn it off, the music stops when you swipe the app away from recents.

3Lyrics

Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB and, if there are none, from KuGou. The app also uses local .lrc files. In the player, tap «Ver completa» (See full) on the lyrics card to see them full screen.

Table 2. Ajustes → Letras (Settings → Lyrics) and Descargas (Downloads)
OptionWhat it does
Mostrar letras (Show lyrics)Shows synced lyrics on the playback screen.
Tamaño del texto (Text size)Size of the lyrics text.
Sincronía de la letra (Lyrics sync)If the lyrics run ahead (e.g. with Bluetooth headphones), raise it.
Guardar letras (Save lyrics)In Ajustes → Descargas: saves lyrics to view them offline.

4Downloads

Download a song from its menu, or a whole album with the download button on its page. To remove it, «Eliminar descarga» (Delete download).

Table 3. Ajustes → Descargas (Settings → Downloads)
OptionWhat it does
Calidad de descarga (Download quality)Quality of the downloaded files (for example, Normal · ~70 kbps · Opus).
Solo con WiFi (Wi-Fi only)Downloads wait until there is Wi-Fi.
Descargar al dar me gusta (Download when liked)Every song you mark with the heart is downloaded automatically.

If you restart your phone with pending downloads, they resume by themselves.

5Quality and battery

Table 4. Ajustes → Sonido (Settings → Sound)
OptionWhat it doesNotes
Calidad de streaming (Streaming quality)Ahorro de datos / Data saver (~50 kbps), Normal (~70 kbps · Opus) or Máxima / Maximum (~160 kbps · Opus).For listening without downloading.
Normalizar volumen (Normalize volume)Raises quiet songs and smooths peaks.Android 9 or later.

6Music on your phone

  1. Open the Local tab and tap «Conceder permiso» (Grant permission) so the app can read your music.
  2. By default it looks through the whole phone. To limit it to a folder: Ajustes → Tus datos → Música del teléfono → Carpeta de música (Music folder). To go back to the whole phone, Usar todo el teléfono (Use the whole phone).
  3. If you add new songs to your phone, in that same menu use Buscar música nueva (Look for new music) (it checks the phone again).

7Library and profiles

  • Likes and playlists: in the Biblioteca (Library) tab. «Crear playlist» (Create playlist) to start one.
  • Importar de YouTube (Import from YouTube) (link icon in the Library): paste the link of a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist or album, public or unlisted (private ones can't be imported). If you already imported it, the new songs are added.
  • Perfiles de gusto (Taste profiles) (Ajustes → Tus datos, or «Principal» on Home): each profile has its own history, recommendations, Likes and playlists. Downloads are shared by all of them. Up to 5 profiles; a new one starts empty and doesn't change what you get in the others.
  • Don't recommend: «No recomendar» from a song's menu. The marked songs are in Ajustes → Tus datos → Canciones no recomendadas (Songs not recommended).

8Backups

  1. Exportar backup (Export backup) (Ajustes → Tus datos): saves your likes, playlists, history, queue and settings to a compressed .extmusic file, wherever you choose.
  2. To move your library to another phone, copy that file and use Importar backup (Import backup): it adds the data of a backup without deleting the current data.
  3. After importing: if you used a music folder, choose it again. The imported queue is already in the player.

Android's automatic backup doesn't include ExtMusic's data: exporting the backup is the only way to save it. More detail in Privacy → What is stored.

9Appearance

  • Tema (Theme): Claro (Light), Oscuro (Dark) or AMOLED (pure black).
  • Fondo con el color de la portada (Background from the cover color): the player, the artist and the album are tinted with the cover's color (in the dark theme).
  • Liquid glass: glass with a magnifier effect on the mini player and the bottom bar. Refraction needs Android 13 or later; on older versions you see a normal blur.

10Updates

In the version downloaded from GitHub, Ajustes → Acerca de (About) has Buscar actualizaciones (Check for updates) and Buscar al abrir la app (Check when opening the app) (at most once a day, without notifying you if there is nothing). When there is a new version, the app downloads it, checks it and asks you to confirm the installation; the music stops for a moment while it installs, and your music, lists and settings stay as they are.

The first time, Android will ask you to allow ExtMusic to install updates; the app uses it only to install its own versions. How each update is verified: Privacy → Verified updates.

11Troubleshooting

Table 5. Common problems
ProblemWhat to do
The music stops when I close the appCheck that Seguir sonando al cerrar la app (Keep playing when the app is closed) is on and allow background playback when the app asks.
The lyrics run aheadRaise Sincronía de la letra (Lyrics sync) in Ajustes → Letras. It happens mostly with Bluetooth headphones.
«No se pudo importar. ¿Es privada o no tienes conexión?» (Couldn't import. Is it private or are you offline?)Check your connection. Private playlists can't be imported; make it public or unlisted.
«Ese enlace no es de una playlist de YouTube» (That link isn't a YouTube playlist)Paste the link of a playlist or album from YouTube or YouTube Music.
After importing a backup my local music is missingChoose the folder again in Ajustes → Tus datos → Música del teléfono → Carpeta de música.
«Ahorro de batería» notice: the audio chip failedThe phone failed to decode with its audio chip and the app saves battery another way. Turn Ahorro de batería (Battery saver) off and on to retry.
The app takes up too much spaceIn Ajustes → Tus datos → Almacenamiento (Storage), lower Máximo de audio temporal (Maximum temporary audio) or use Liberar espacio (Free up space) (it doesn't touch downloads, Likes or playlists).
«La descarga no coincidía con la publicada…» or «La APK no era válida…» (the download didn't match / the APK wasn't valid)The app has already deleted the file for safety. Tap to retry; if it happens again, check your connection or download the APK from Versions.
«No se pudo actualizar. Revisa tu conexión.» on Home (Couldn't refresh. Check your connection.)Check your connection and pull down on Home to refresh.

12Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google account?
No. ExtMusic has no accounts and doesn't sign in to YouTube.
Is it free?
Yes. It is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later, with no ads or purchases.
Is it available in English?
The app is currently in Spanish only. This website is available in Spanish and English.
Does it work offline?
Yes, with downloaded songs and with the music on your phone. Searching and listening to YouTube Music need a connection.
Where is it published?
For now, only on GitHub Releases. Copies from other sites are not official.
Is it affiliated with YouTube or Google?
No, nor endorsed by them. It uses NewPipeExtractor to get the audio from YouTube Music, so it depends on YouTube remaining accessible that way.
What data does it send?
No analytics. The services it connects to and what they receive are in Privacy → Network connections.
