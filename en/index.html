1Description

ExtMusic

A free player for YouTube Music without an account and for the music stored on your phone.

Download APK v1.4.2 · 4.8 MB Source code

Android 8.0+ · GPL-3.0 · No account · No ads · No analytics

Search and listen to the YouTube Music catalog without signing in, download songs for offline listening and play your local files. Your library is stored only on your phone. The app is currently available in Spanish only. Every feature is listed in Table 1.

3What's new in version 1.4.2

Released 2026-11-25 · official release notes

  • New: the app tells you when there's a new version and installs it for you
  • Fix: downloads no longer get stuck on some Wi-Fi networks
  • Fix: a cut-off download no longer leaves a broken song
  • Asks permission to show notifications the first time

How updating works: the app downloads the new version from GitHub, checks its SHA-256 fingerprint and its signature, and opens the Android installer for you to confirm. Only in the version downloaded from GitHub.
